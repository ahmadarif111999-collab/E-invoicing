# Compliance notes

- FBR integration is mocked in this starter.
- Do not claim official FBR certification unless actually approved.
- Do not claim licensed-integrator status without proof.
- HS/PCT suggestions are not final legal classification.
- CD% is customs duty reference from the tariff source, not sales tax.
- Real production FBR submission must be implemented through official API access or a licensed integrator.
- Submitted invoice edits/cancellations must follow applicable FBR rules and must be audit logged.
